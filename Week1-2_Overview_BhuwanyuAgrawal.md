# Week 1–2 Overview | Bhuwanyu Agrawal (2025)

A compilation of two weeks of creative coding projects made using **p5.js**, exploring emotion, generative design, interactivity, and narrative storytelling.  
Each project builds upon the previous one—progressing from geometric emotion studies to interactive narrative experiences.

## **Week 1**

### **Day 1 – Visualizing Anger**

**Overview:**  
A digital composition expressing *anger* through sharp, overlapping triangles in red and black.  
The piece explores geometric abstraction and emotional tension using color, form, and composition.

### **Day 2 – From Anger to Wonder**

**Overview:**  
An animated extension of *Visualizing Anger*, representing emotional transformation from chaos to clarity.  
Utilizes variables to animate triangle growth, fading, and color transitions.

### **Day 3 – 10PRINT Pattern**

**Overview:**  
A generative art experiment inspired by the classic *10PRINT* pattern.  
Explores randomness, loops, and structure to create unpredictable geometric visuals.  
Includes four 50×50 px hand-drawn repeating patterns created in Procreate.

**Links:**

### **Day 4 – Generative Image Pattern Exploration**

**Overview:**  
An ongoing experiment exploring repetition, variation, and arrays in generative art.  
Uses handmade motifs and tiling logic to create potential continuous patterns.

**Overview:**  
A sprite-based animation project implementing a custom Samurai Platformer sprite sheet.  
Includes walking, sprinting, jumping, and attacking controls, plus a day–night cycle using background color transition.
